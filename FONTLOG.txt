FONTLOG for the Pusto Pin font
==============================

This file provides detailed information on the Pusto Pin font family.
This information should be distributed along with the Pusto Pin fonts
and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Pusto Pin is a rounded unicase stroke typeface by Pustota, supporting
Latin and Cyrillic. It was drawn for the Pusto pins. It is developed in
Glyphs and distributed here in OTF and web (WOFF, WOFF2) formats.


ChangeLog
---------

18 Nov 2026 (Pustota)  Pusto Pin v0.007
- Spacing pass: sidebearings of letters and figures set from the white
  area at each edge, calibrated on H (70) and O (55)

18 Nov 2026 (Pustota)  Pusto Pin v0.006
- Cyrillic look-alikes (A, Ve, Ka, En, O, Er, Es, Te, Ha) are now
  components of Latin; Latin E and M use the Cyrillic drawings
- Zhe and Ef use the common 180 stroke
- Round letters and figures overshoot by 10 units, flat ones sit on 0-700

17 Nov 2026 (Pustota)  Pusto Pin v0.005
- Fixed sidebearings of Cyrillic U, Zhe and Ie
- Raised the dieresis of Io and shortened the hook of the question mark,
  so the gaps print on a 0.4 mm nozzle

17 Nov 2026 (Pustota)  Pusto Pin v0.004
- Renamed from the working title "PP Pusto"
- Added .notdef (peace sign)
- Aligned straight letters to the 0-700 cap height
- Unfinished Georgian glyphs excluded from export
- First public repository, OFL 1.1
